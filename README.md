Ever found yourself scrambling to meet an important deadline that wasn’t on your professor’s syllabus? In this practical, focused course, you’ll take charge of the academic and professional milestones that matter most like certificate applications and e-portfolio development without waiting for reminders. Use or create a clear, actionable system to identify, schedule, and complete key responsibilities, and discover how small, consistent actions can keep you on track for graduation and allow you to receive your graduate certificates on time. Build habits that serve you throughout your program and beyond, so you can move confidently toward your goals.
